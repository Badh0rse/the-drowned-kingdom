# The Drowned Kingdom

A free, hand-inked horror life sim set on the Isle of Carrow: a fishing island with 30 days left before something old wakes beneath the harbor.

**Play it in your browser:** https://badh0rse.github.io/the-drowned-kingdom/

Raise a family and make friends and enemies in a town of people who remember you. Fish the canals, dive the drowned caves below, fight through candle-lit mines and flooded catacombs, and decide what you will offer the Harbor Idol to buy the island a little more time.

Free to play, always. If you enjoy it, you can support development on Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/cosmerecrawler

The music is an original score generated as you play. Credits for the free (CC0) models and sounds are in [CREDITS.txt](CREDITS.txt). three.js is used under the MIT license ([LICENSE-three.js.txt](LICENSE-three.js.txt)).
